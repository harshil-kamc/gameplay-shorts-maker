# Skill: Video Editing (gameplay shorts)

Turning raw AAA PC gameplay into a finished vertical short.

- **Frame the action:** keep the gameplay subject, vehicle, boss, or HUD readable. Fit the full game frame when the action is wide; punch in only on the action that matters.
- **Trim dead air:** cut idle walking, menu time, silences > 0.4s, and setup before the action lands.
- **Open on the hook frame** — never on a neutral resting shot. Start on motion, impact, danger, or a HUD change.
- **Visual rhythm:** something should change every 2–3 seconds (game cut, zoom, caption emphasis, highlight, SFX) to reset attention.
- **Captions are mandatory** — most viewers watch muted. Big, word-synced, high-contrast.
- **Safe zones:** keep text clear of the top ~12% and bottom ~18% of the frame.
- **Consistency:** same caption style, font, and intro treatment across a series builds a brand.
- **Export:** 1080×1920, H.264, yuv420p, ~8–12 Mbps, AAC audio. Loudness around -14 LUFS.

## 2026 research addendum
- **Crop to the ACTION, not a face.** Keep the game element that carries the story visible; never cut away from the payoff to show a face-cam reaction.
- **The 2.5-second law:** mobile attention for a static shot is ~2.8s. Change the screen every ≤2.5s: cut, punch, caption pop, highlight, or impact SFX.
- **85% of short-form is watched MUTED.** Captions + visible game state are the primary video track; native game audio is the bonus.
- **Clean beats busy.** Remove dead space and make each second intentional. Random overlays over gameplay read as SLOP and get roasted.

## The million-view visual grammar
- **BURST-THEN-HOLD rhythm:** fast cuts through the hook, then longer holds through the payoff if the action is still moving.
- **The relentless push-in:** long holds are never static — a slow zoom creep toward the action keeps motion alive.
- **1-2 word caption pages; NUMBERS PAGE ALONE**: health, ammo, speed, or damage numbers deserve their own frame.
- **Impact frames carry the payoff:** the crash, kill, save, or glitch gets the cleanest cut and the loudest native sound.
- Zero decorative overlays. Gameplay + captions + rhythm + real impact — that's the kit.
