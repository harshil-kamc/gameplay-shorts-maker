# Skill: Growth Strategy — Views & Subscribers (2026 data-backed)

Concrete targets for turning AAA PC gameplay clips into views and subscribers.

## TEAM DOCTRINE
- **We are a gameplay channel, not a podcast curator.** Use our own gameplay footage. Every post needs an editorial layer — a clear take, question, comparison, or outrageous in-game moment — never just "here's the clip."
- **Title formula:** GAME/SET PIECE + emotional verb or question, front-loaded, ≤60 chars. "FLIPS A CAR INTO THE FINAL BOSS" / "How did he survive that crash?"
- **Niche-lock:** AAA PC gameplay. Keep the algorithm's signal consistent: gameplay, gaming, PC gaming, game clips.

## Length: 20–45 seconds is the sweet spot
- **Sub-15s Shorts collapsed in reach** in 2026 — they cannot clear the absolute watch-time bar even at 100% retention.
- **20–45s with one complete action beat** is the target. Go longer only if every second raises the stakes.

## Retention is the gate
- Structure every clip: **visible hook (0–1.5s) → escalation → payoff**. Most weak clips die in the setup because nothing moves.
- Engineer a loop where the final action flows back into the opening frame.
- Change the visual every ~2s with cuts, zooms, highlights, or on-screen text.

## Engagement drives the test window
- Every clip should end on a comment-trigger: "Would you survive that?", "Was that a glitch?", "Best escape you've seen?".
- Reply to early comments — comments are one of the strongest 2026 signals.
- Shares and saves matter too.

## Posting cadence
- Consistency beats bursts. Never ship a weak clip just to hit a number; one strong gameplay moment > five mediocre ones.
- Shorts recruit; longer gameplay videos convert viewers into subscribers and watch hours.

## Small-channel edge
- Use the game's native audio/impact sounds and your own voice when present. Don't bury the moment under loud music.
- A recognizable game/title in the caption and title helps search and recommendation routing.

## Niche consistency
- Stay in AAA PC gameplay. Avoid drifting into unrelated podcast, interview, or reacting-to-other-content clips.
