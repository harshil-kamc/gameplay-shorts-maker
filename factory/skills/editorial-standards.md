# Editorial Standards — the channel's quality bar

Written for AAA PC GAMEPLAY shorts. Every agent applies the part that touches its job. The Manager uses this as the pre-upload review checklist — a clip that violates a MUST gets held back.

## FINDER — what makes a clip worth cutting
- MUST: the visible game action is the story. Pick combat, chases, crashes, stunts, boss fights, close calls, glitches, funny in-game failures, big wins, and cinematic set pieces.
- MUST: the moment works muted — motion, impact, HUD changes, or a clear stake is obvious without audio.
- MUST: the first 1.5s shows action already landing. No wind-up, menu, inventory, idle walking, loading screen, or creator face-cam with no gameplay.
- MUST: candidate clips are 18-90s; target 20-45s. Final rendered clips must be at least 15s.
- MUST: cutscenes and scripted dialogue scenes are rejected. A dramatic line is not gameplay.
  Require visible player-controlled action, a playable camera, or a gameplay HUD.
- PREFER: visible HUD/health/ammo changes, danger, near-death saves, a clean cause-and-effect payoff, and a comment-triggering hot take.
- NEVER: treat this as a podcast/interview or avoid screen content — the screen content IS the video.

## EDITOR — how it must look and sound
- MUST: never cover gameplay with b-roll or stock overlays. The game footage is the story. Add information with captions, highlights, zooms, and hook text instead.
- MUST: keep the HUD, vehicle, weapon, boss, or action readable when it matters; crop only when it improves the gameplay subject.
- MUST: hook text on screen in the first 0.5s and gone by ~2.2s; 1-3 word caption pages, bold ALL-CAPS, key word highlighted.
- MUST: captions stay inside frame and never bury the action or HUD element that makes the clip understandable.
- MUST: change what is on screen every ~2s using real gameplay cuts, punch-ins on action, speed ramps, highlights, or SFX.
- MUST: audio is clean and loudness ~-14 LUFS; game audio is the bed, music sits under it and never masks impact sounds.

## UPLOADER — packaging
- MUST: the title promises exactly the in-game moment delivered — game/event first, emotional verb second.
- MUST: title ≤ 95 chars, front-loaded, caption ends with a comment-bait question or hot take.
- PREFER: gameplay hashtags (#gaming, #gameplay, #gamingshorts, #pcgaming), not podcast tags.

## MANAGER — review checklist (pre-upload gate)
Mechanically verify, then judge:
1. File exists, final duration 15-60s, loudness sane.
2. Title/hook/caption match the visible gameplay (no overpromise).
3. Standards above: would a stranger understand and stay for 3 seconds?
4. Verdict: approve, or BOUNCE with 1-3 specific, actionable notes.
Never let a clip that fails a MUST go out silently.
