# Skill: Viral Patterns

Recurring shapes of gameplay clips that overperform. Use as a checklist when scoring moments.

- **The chaos payoff** — setup is chaos, payoff is an even bigger crash/collapse/explosion.
- **The impossible save** — death looks certain, then a last-second dodge, catch, or shot.
- **The grand theft grand plan** — a ridiculous vehicle/heist/stunt idea executes surprisingly well or fails spectacularly.
- **The boss humbling** — overconfidence meets a brutal boss, then a comeback or a humiliating death.
- **The physics moment** — ragdoll, vehicle, or NPC behavior does something absurdly funny.
- **The glitch reveal** — the game breaks in a way viewers have to see to believe.
- **The cinematic accident** — an accidental moment looks like a scripted movie shot.
- **The speedrun brain** — an unexpectedly clever shortcut, trick, or exploit.
- **The "wait, what?" in-game beat** — the action flips expectations in under a second.

**Avoid (these usually flop as gameplay clips):**
- Menus, inventory management, map screens, loadouts, and idle walking.
- Cutscenes, scripted dialogue, and cinematic scenes with no playable camera or HUD.
- Long commentary with no visible action or stake.
- Quiet face-cam segments with the game minimized or ignored.
- Anything requiring prior context from the stream.
- Clips under 15s — they cannot clear the watch-time bar.
